# Code of conduct

Steppy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions or any other project space, is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and considerate with people, assume good faith, criticise
code and ideas rather than the people who wrote them, and keep harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language out of every project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, write to the maintainers privately at the
contact address on the [Steppy-dev organisation profile](https://github.com/Steppy-dev).
Do not open a public issue for it. Reports are read only by the maintainers and
are kept confidential.

Security vulnerabilities are a different channel: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide how to respond to a report, following the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, a temporary ban, or a
permanent ban from project spaces, depending on what happened and whether it
has happened before. A maintainer involved in a report takes no part in
handling it.
